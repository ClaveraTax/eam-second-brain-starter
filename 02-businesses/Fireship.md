# Fireship

A developer channel with quick, opinionated rundowns of new tools. Plenty of open-source AI.

> [!TIP]
> **Start with**
>
> [5 open source tools that replaced my $320mo AI stack](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y5rSSvXfL4g)

## Their angle

- Every tool gets a sentence on what it does and what it replaces.
- Leans hard towards open source and self-hosting.

> [!WARNING]
> **Worth knowing**
>
> Sponsor segments are woven into the videos, and the setups assume you can install anything.
