# Example — Head of Finance

> Fictional. A template showing what a useful stakeholder note looks like.

**In one line:** wants to close the books two days faster without adding headcount.

## Cares about
- Close cycle time, audit findings, cost per invoice
- Not being surprised by IT spend mid-year

## How they decide
- Numbers first, story second. Wants a baseline and a payback period on one slide.
- Trusts the external auditor's view more than any vendor's.

## Language that lands / language that doesn't
- Lands: "fewer manual reconciliations", "audit trail", "pilot with one entity".
- Doesn't: "AI transformation", "platform", "agents". All sound like open-ended cost.
