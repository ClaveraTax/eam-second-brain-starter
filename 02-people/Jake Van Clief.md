# Jake Van Clief

Argues that your folders are the AI system and the tools come second. This repo borrows his structure.

> [!TIP]
> **Start with**
>
> [Stop Buying Agent Tools. Your Folders Already Do This](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KFmnlS9Y3qo)

## How they think

- One well-organised library and one good assistant beat a swarm of agents.
- Router files tell the assistant where to look.
- Numbered stages, with a human check between them.
