# Jake Van Clief

Teaches that your folders are the AI system; tools come second. This repo follows his approach.

## How they think
- One well-organised library and one good assistant beat a swarm of agents.
- Router files point the assistant to the right folder.
- Numbered stages with stopping points, so a human checks between steps.
