# 02-people — stage 2: how your stakeholders think

One note per person you need to understand, convince or support. The goal is to prepare better, not to keep a file on someone.

- Use [`_template.md`](_template.md) or [`../skills/write-a-person-note.md`](../skills/write-a-person-note.md).
- **Write what helps the work:** priorities, decision style, language that lands. Leave out private life, health, gossip and anything you wouldn't be comfortable with them reading.
- Check your company's rules on keeping notes about colleagues (GDPR applies to these notes too).
- **The two notes here are fictional examples.**
