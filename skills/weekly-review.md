# Skill: weekly review

**Use when:** every Friday, 15 minutes.
**Touches:** `00-inbox/`, then proposes changes everywhere.

---

Copy everything below the line into your assistant. Paste the contents of `00-inbox/` and the list of files in `02-people/` and `03-briefs/` after it. If your assistant can read the folder directly, just point it at the folder.

---

Run my weekly second-brain review. Propose; don't act.

1. **Inbox.** For each item in `00-inbox/`, propose one of: *make a source note*, *add to person note <name>*, *add to brief <name>*, or *delete*. One line each, with a reason.
2. **People.** List open threads older than two weeks in `02-people/`. Who do I owe something to?
3. **Briefs.** List briefs with `status: draft` that have a date in the past or next week.
4. **One pattern.** Name one thing I did more than twice this week that could become a new file in `skills/`. Just one, or none.

Format as a short checklist. Then stop and wait for me to say which proposals to carry out.
