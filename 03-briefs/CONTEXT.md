# 03-briefs — stage 3: what you use

The output stage: meeting prep, decision memos, one-pagers, talking points. A brief is built **from** notes in `01-sources/` and `02-people/` and links back to them.

- Ask your assistant: *"Using `02-people/<person>.md` and the sources it links, draft a one-page brief for my meeting on <topic>."*
- **Gate:** you edit and approve every brief before it leaves your hands.
- **Name files** by purpose, e.g. `Pilot pitch to the CISO.md`.
- The example here is fictional.
