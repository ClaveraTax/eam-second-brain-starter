# Maps of Content

Hub notes that collect links on one topic, so you find things by meaning rather than by folder.

- Worth adding once a topic has more than five or six notes.

> [!TIP]
> **Try it**
>
> Open this folder in Obsidian and switch on graph view to see the links.
