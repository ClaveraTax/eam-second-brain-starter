# Write a person note

Write a short note on how this person or company thinks, from their public work only.

1. A natural person goes in `02-people/`; a company, lab or channel goes in `02-businesses/`.
2. One line on what they teach or do, three bullets on how they think.
3. List their sources with a link to watch or read, and link the concepts they cover.
4. No private life, health, politics or gossip.
5. Use the folder's `_template.md`, file name on the first line, then stop and wait for edits.
