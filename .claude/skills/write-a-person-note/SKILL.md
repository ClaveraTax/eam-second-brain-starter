# Write a person note

> [!TIP]
> **Not using Claude Code?**
>
> Paste this file into your assistant, then paste your material.

Write a short note on how this person or company thinks, using their public work only.

1. A person goes in `02-people/`. A company, lab or channel goes in `02-businesses/`.
2. One line on what they teach or do, then a couple of lines on how they see things.
3. List their sources with a link to watch or read, and link the concepts they cover.
4. Leave out private life, health, politics and gossip.
5. Follow the folder's `_template.md`, put the file name on the first line, then wait for edits.
