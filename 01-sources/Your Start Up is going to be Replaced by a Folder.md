# Your Start Up is going to be Replaced by a Folder

You don't need hundreds of agents. You need a well-kept library and one good librarian.

> [!NOTE]
> **Source**
>
> [Watch on YouTube (12 min)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XIk-Ru85xmA)

## Key points

- Short router notes work like a card catalog. They tell the model where to look instead of handing it every book.
- Sharing a brain across a company is the hard part. That's where access control, governance and monitoring come in.
