# Stop Buying Agent Tools. Your Folders Already Do This

If your folders are well organised, they already are the instructions an AI assistant needs.

> [!NOTE]
> **Source**
>
> [Watch on YouTube (11 min)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KFmnlS9Y3qo)

## Key points

- When the folder holds the context, you don't need a long prompt. Point the assistant at the folder.
- He calls "agent" a user-experience label: a name we give automations so we can keep track of them.
- Stages with stopping points decide where the AI waits for you. He'd rather augment than automate everything.

> [!WARNING]
> **Read with care**
>
> The "~97% fewer tokens" figure is his own estimate, not a measurement.
